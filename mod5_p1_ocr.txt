* As an example, a common objective in quantization error analysis is to choose the digital word
fength such that the digital system is a sufficiently accurate rentization of the desired linear system
and at the same time, requires a minimum of hardware or software complexity. The digital word
jegnth can, of course, be changed only in steps of 1 bit. and the addition of 1 bit to the word length
reduces the size of quantization errors by a factor of 2. Thus, the choice of word length is insensitive
to inaccuracies in the quantization error analysis; an analysis that is correct to within 30-40% is
often adequate. For this reason, many of the important effects of quantization can be studied using
linear models.
