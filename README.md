## 🎯 Syllabus Filtering Applied

* ❌ **Completely Dropped (Out of syllabus / never tested):** Composite Radix FFT ($N = 2 \cdot 3, 3 \cdot 2$), Chebyshev-II, Elliptical (Cauer) filters.
* ⬇️ **De-prioritized / Excluded:** Digital resonators, digital sinusoidal oscillators, Chebyshev-I approximations.
* ⭐ **Highest Priority Focus:**
  1. **IIR Realizations** (Direct Form I & II, Cascade, Parallel Form with partial fractions).
  2. **Butterworth Filter Design & BLT / IIM Transformations**.
  3. **FIR Filter Realizations** (Folded linear-phase minimum multipliers structure).
  4. **Radix-2 FFT** (DIT and DIF 8-point butterfly calculations).
  5. **Finite Word Length Effects** (ADC noise power, Limit cycles deadband formula, $L_2$ scaling).
